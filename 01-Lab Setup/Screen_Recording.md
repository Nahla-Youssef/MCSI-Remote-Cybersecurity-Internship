# Lab Setup: Screen Recording
___

## References:
### OBS Studio (official download): https://obsproject.com/download
___
### 1-Install Screen Recording Software:

1.1-Choose a tool (e.g., TinyTake or OBS).

1.2-Install it following official instructions.

---
### 2-Set Up Recording Environment:

2.1-Launch the software and review its options.

2.2-Configure settings: full screen capture, audio disabled, 720p+ quality.
___
### 3-Prepare Submission Materials:

3.1-Open the MCSI platform in your browser.

3.2-Create an intro slide/file with your Student ID and exercise title.

3.3-Create a closing slide/file thanking the instructor.
___
### 4-Record the Video:

4.1-Record your intro file.

4.2-Navigate through: Videos → Library → FAQ ("Need Help?") → Portfolio

4.3-Bookmark an exercise.

4.4-Record your closing thank-you file.
___
### 5-Upload to YouTube:

5.1-Log in to YouTube.

5.2-Upload the video as Unlisted.

5.3-Copy the shareable link.
___
